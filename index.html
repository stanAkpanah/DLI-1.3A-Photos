Add new captures

Add the photos your team sent on WhatsApp: choose them, drag them in, or paste them. Then fill in the form number, name and phone from the WhatsApp caption, check them, and save. Each one is filed under its form number.

Fastest way: import the whole WhatsApp chat at once
  1. On your phone, open the WhatsApp chat (or group) where the team sends captures.
  2. Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat → Include media. iPhone: tap the chat name → Export Chat → Attach Media.
  3. Save the export to your phone (Files or Drive), or send it to yourself.
  4. Tap Import WhatsApp chat and choose the .zip (or the .txt plus the photos). Each photo is matched with its caption, sender and date. Captures already in the registry are skipped, so you can import the same chat again each day.
